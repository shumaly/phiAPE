# Figure 1

`phiAPE_positional_similarity.png` is the original Figure 1 asset from the
main WSDM 2027 manuscript. It compares the positional similarity of Sin-APE,
tAPE, and phiAPE for sequence lengths 25 and 400 and embedding widths 32
and 128. The curves and heatmaps are calculated directly from the frequency
schedules.
